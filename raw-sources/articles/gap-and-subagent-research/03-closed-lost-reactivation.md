# RAW: Closed-Lost Pipeline Reactivation Framework
Segment lost deals by LOSS REASON, not uniformly (budget freeze != leadership change response).
3 trigger categories to monitor: regulatory changes creating urgency, internal shifts (leadership/funding), champion movement to new company (tracking champions alone drove 30% of annual revenue at one company cited).
4D architecture: Data (identify closed-lost + trigger events) -> Distribution (right channel) -> Destination (contextual content, not just a booking link) -> Direction (measure/optimize).
Standardized reactivation sequences per loss category (competitor migration, missed meetings, stalled deals) beat generic "checking in" messages -- must connect fresh trigger context to "why now."
