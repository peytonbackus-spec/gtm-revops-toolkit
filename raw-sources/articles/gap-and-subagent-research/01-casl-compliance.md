# RAW: CASL Rules for Cold B2B Email to Canadian Prospects
Implied consent is extremely limited -- only applies with an existing business relationship (prior purchase/signed contract). Cold outreach with no prior relationship generally requires EXPRESS consent (a direct yes -- checked box, submitted form).
Required in every commercial electronic message: sender's real name, company name, physical address, working phone/email; functional one-click unsubscribe honored within 10 business days; honest non-deceptive subject lines; no identity masking; disclose if automated.
Record-keeping: every consent must be recorded with timestamp + IP address for audit defense.
Penalties: up to $1,000,000 CAD per violation, enforced by the CRTC; single complaint can trigger investigation; domain blocking/blacklisting risk.
DIRECTLY RELEVANT: Peyton's consulting firm is Ontario-registered and its core service involves cold outbound (Apollo/Clay/sequences) for B2B clients -- this is a real compliance exposure, not a hypothetical.
