# RAW: "Creating Effective Customer Success Playbooks"
Lifecycle stages: Onboarding (contract->first value), Adoption (usage/feature engagement), Renewal (proactive), Advocacy (champions/case studies), Offboarding (professional account closure). Plus specialized: QBR, new product rollout.
Structure: task modules broken into steps, clear objectives/success outcomes per phase, assigned ownership, milestone tracking, customer segmentation (high-touch vs low-touch), embedded resources (videos/templates/FAQs).
Iteration: measure completion rates + downstream outcomes (retention, NPS, expansion), refine from CSM feedback.
