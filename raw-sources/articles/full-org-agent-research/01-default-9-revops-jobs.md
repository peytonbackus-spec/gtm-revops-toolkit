# RAW: "AI for RevOps: 9 Jobs to Automate Across the Revenue Cycle"
1. Qualify inbound leads (Inbound SDR) - enrich firmographics, ICP compare, dedupe, ownership, scoring before human review
2. Enrich customer records (RevOps) - waterfall enrichment during intake, reconcile conflicts, flag anomalies
3. Score/prioritize leads (Inbound SDR) - dynamic scoring from real-time signals (site activity, email engagement, intent)
4. Keep CRM records updated (RevOps) - summarize calls, capture next steps, update stages, flag missing fields, create tasks
5. Personalize outreach (Outbound SDR) - route by intent, AI-score against past conversations + external data for optimal timing
6. Automate RFP/proposal generation (AE/Presales) - pull past responses + legal language + KB into first drafts
7. Watch pipeline continuously (RevOps) - detect stalled deals, missing next steps, declining engagement, unexpected stage moves
8. Prepare sellers for conversations (AE) - account briefs from interactions + usage + support tickets + buying committee + competitive intel
9. Coordinate revenue workflows (RevOps) - end-to-end: qualify -> enrich -> dedupe -> route -> schedule -> update CRM -> trigger downstream
