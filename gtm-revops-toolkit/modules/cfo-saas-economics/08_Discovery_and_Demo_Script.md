# Executive Discovery & Demo Script

---

## First 5 Minutes: High-Impact Discovery Questions

1. *"How do you currently handle foreign currency supplier wires or offshore payroll cycles today?"*
2. *"When your treasury team converts CAD to USD, EUR, or local currency, what spread or fee is your bank charging on top of the mid-market rate?"*
3. *"How often do supplier shipments or contractor payouts get delayed by traditional 3-to-5 day SWIFT wire holds?"*

---

## The "Ah-Ha" Demo Sequence

* **Minute 0–2:** Log into the Kavodax Enterprise Portal. Highlight real-time treasury balances.
* **Minute 2–4:** Initiate a live test payout (e.g., $500 CAD settlement) to a test corridor account.
* **Minute 4–5:** Show real-time on-chain confirmation and local fiat delivery arriving in sub-60 seconds. Point out the zero wire fee and 0.5% fixed spread.

---

## Closing Framework: The Pilot Corridor Trial

> *"We don't expect you to move your entire treasury operation overnight. Let's select one recurring payment corridor—such as your bi-weekly offshore software developer payroll or a $20,000 supplier invoice—and run a single pilot transaction next week. You'll compare the exact execution speed and cost side-by-side against your primary bank."*
