# 04 — Zero-to-One 90-Day GTM Roadmap (For CEO Gharsa Amin)

## Days 1–30: Foundation & Signal Deployment
* Audit existing prospect conversations and refine early buyer personas.
* Deploy outbound signal triggers for automated account scoring.
* Establish baseline metrics for response rates and discovery meetings booked.

## Days 31–60: Outbound Acceleration & Pilot Conversion
* Execute targeted outbound campaigns across key ICP accounts.
* Standardize a 14-day evaluation playbook to transition prospects into paid pilots.
* Establish feedback loops between sales discovery and product development.

## Days 61–90: Revenue Scale & Process Standardization
* Build repeatable outbound playbooks for future sales hires.
* Optimize pilot-to-contract conversion workflows.
* Present initial quarterly pipeline review and growth projections to leadership.
