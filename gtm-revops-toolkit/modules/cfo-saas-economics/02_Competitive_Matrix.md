# Competitive Positioning & Moat Analysis

## Differentiators by Competitor Type

### 1. Vs. Traditional Canadian Banks (Tier 1 Banks)
* **The Bank Gap:** Banks charge 2.5% to 4.0% FX spreads plus $30–$50 wire fees, taking up to 5 days.
* **Kavodax Edge:** Same-day / sub-5 minute finality with sub-1% total cost.

### 2. Vs. Legacy Payment Fintechs (Wise, OFX)
* **The Fintech Gap:** Wise relies on traditional correspondent banking rails and local pool liquidity, limiting transaction speed on large B2B payments ($100k+).
* **Kavodax Edge:** CAGD provides 24/7/365 instant on-chain liquidity settlement without correspondent banking friction.

### 3. Vs. Offshore Crypto / USD Stablecoins (USDT / USDC)
* **The Crypto Gap:** Offshore stablecoins lack Canadian FINTRAC/Bank of Canada registration and introduce double-FX exposure (CAD → USD → Fiat).
* **Kavodax Edge:** 1:1 CAD backing eliminates double-FX risk and gives corporate risk committees complete regulatory compliance assurance.
