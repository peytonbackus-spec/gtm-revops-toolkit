## Key Regulatory Protections

### 1. FINTRAC Registration & MSB Oversight
* **Status:** Fully registered Money Services Business (MSB) under FINTRAC regulations.
* **Compliance:** Adheres to strict Canadian Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Counter-Terrorist Financing (CTF) protocols.

### 3. Reserve Transparency & 1:1 Peg Auditability
* **Backing:** Every CAGD token in circulation is backed 1:1 by Canadian Dollar fiat deposits and short-term Government of Canada treasury bills held in custodian accounts.
* **Attestations:** Regular independent third-party audit attestations confirm full reserve ratio coverage.

## Security & Treasury Risk FAQ

* **Do we hold crypto assets on our balance sheet?**
  * *No.* CAGD acts purely as a transient settlement rail. Funds convert from CAD → CAGD → Local Target Fiat within minutes, eliminating cryptocurrency price volatility exposure.

* **How are funds cleared into local destination bank accounts?**
  * Destination partners execute instant local clearing house payouts directly to supplier/contractor bank accounts.
