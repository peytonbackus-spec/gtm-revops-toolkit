# Ideal Customer Profiles (ICPs) & Target Segments

To achieve $Millions in ARR, commercial focus must target mid-market businesses transferring high volume on consistent schedules, rather than low-value retail transactions.

---

## Tier 1 ICP: Canadian Importers & Exporters

* **Target Industries:** Agriculture, Food & Beverage, Auto Parts Manufacturing, Consumer Electronics.
* **Company Size:** $5M – $50M annual revenue ($500k+ in monthly cross-border payments).
* **Primary Decision Makers:** CFO, VP Finance, Director of Procurement.
* **Core Pain Point:** Trapped cash flow, 3–4% FX markups, and delayed supplier release due to wire holds (SWIFT).
* **Target Corridors:** Canada ↔ LATAM (Mexico, Colombia), Canada ↔ Southeast Asia (Vietnam, Philippines), Canada ↔ West Africa (Nigeria, Ghana).

---

## Tier 2 ICP: Cross-Border Software & Agency Payroll

* **Target Industries:** IT Consultancies, Software Dev Agencies, Remote Engineering Hubs.
* **Company Size:** 20–200 international contractors or offshore staff.
* **Primary Decision Makers:** Head of HR/People, VP Operations, Chief Operating Officer.
* **Core Pain Point:** Paying high per-seat transaction fees and FX markups on platforms like Wise, Deel, or traditional bank wires every bi-weekly cycle.
* **CAGD Solution:** Bulk automated payouts directly to contractor wallets via CAGD rails in sub-5 minutes.
