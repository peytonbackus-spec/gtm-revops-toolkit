# 02 — Founder-Led Sales Playbook

## Executive Discovery Framework
* **Current State:** "How are your reps currently identifying and prioritizing high-intent outbound accounts?"
* **Operational Impact:** "What is the cost of low pipeline velocity on your quarterly revenue targets?"
* **Value Alignment:** "How would automating signal-based outreach impact your team's quota attainment?"

## Objection Handling
* **"We already have an established outbound tool stack."** $\rightarrow$ *"We complement existing CRMs by driving real-time intent signals directly into your workflow to boost conversion rates."*
* **"We are holding off on new software spending this quarter."** $\rightarrow$ *"Our initial focus is running a short audit to identify immediate pipeline opportunities that pay for the platform."*
