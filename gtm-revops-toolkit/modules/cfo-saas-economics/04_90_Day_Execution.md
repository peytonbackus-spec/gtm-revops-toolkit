# 90-Day Commercial Execution Roadmap

## Phase 1: Days 1–30 | Foundation & Pipeline Audit
* **Pipeline Assessment:** Audit existing leads; categorize by high-volume corridors (Canada ↔ West Africa, LATAM, South Asia).
* **Messaging Optimization:** Refine sales collateral away from "crypto jargon" toward CFO ROI metrics (speed, cost savings, compliance).
* **Compliance Handshake:** Establish a streamlined KYC/AML onboarding workflow with compliance.

## Phase 2: Days 31–60 | Outbound Engine & Channel Partnerships
* **Direct Outbound:** Launch targeted outreach campaigns to CFOs/VPs of Finance in Canadian manufacturing, agriculture, and tech sectors ($5M–$50M ARR).
* **Channel Distribution:** Partner with Canadian Trade Associations, Freight Forwarders, and Offshore IT Recruiting Agencies to establish referral loops.

## Phase 3: Days 61–90 | Scale & Repeatable Playbook
* **Playbook Standardization:** Document high-converting email sequences, objection handling, and ROI calculators into a repeatable sales deck.
* **Hiring Roadmap:** Map out the first sales hire profile (Account Executive / SDR) as transaction volume scales past founder-led capacity.
