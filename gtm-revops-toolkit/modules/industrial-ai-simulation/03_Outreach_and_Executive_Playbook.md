## Executive Discovery Framework

1. **Current State & Bottlenecks:**
   * "How are your engineering teams currently handling thermal and structural validation across design iterations?"
   * "Where do handover friction or data silos occur between your design teams and PLM/Teamcenter?"

2. **Quantifying Operational Impact:**
   * "What is the cost impact on your launch timelines when physical prototype testing uncovers unexpected thermal issues late in the cycle?"
   * "How are you leveraging operational shop-floor data to feed predictive maintenance models?"

3. **Objection Handling:**
   * **"We already use Ansys / PTC."** $\rightarrow$ *"We frequently work alongside existing tools, but our team specializes in bridging multi-physics simulation directly into Teamcenter and custom AI models to drastically reduce solve times."*
   * **"We don't have budget for new software this quarter."** $\rightarrow$ *"Understood—our initial focus is usually auditing existing engineering workflows to identify where productivity savings can self-fund future optimization."*
