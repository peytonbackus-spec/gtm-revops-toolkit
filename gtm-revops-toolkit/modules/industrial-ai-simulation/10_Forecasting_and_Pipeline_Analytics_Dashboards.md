# 10 — Forecasting & Pipeline Analytics Dashboards

## Core Executive Metrics & KPIs (For CRO Tim Lavell)
1. **Pipeline Coverage & Velocity:** Stage-by-stage conversion rate, deal cycle duration, and average deal size by vertical.
2. **Siemens Co-Sell Multiplier:** Revenue win rate when co-selling with Siemens reps vs. standalone Maya HTT deals.
3. **AI IP Attach Rate:** Percentage of enterprise software deals that include custom Maya HTT IP (EPIQ-M / M-Bot) or engineering services.

## Automated Dashboard Architecture
* **Salesforce Native Dashboards:** Real-time visibility into rep activity, pipeline health, and commit vs. best-case forecasting.
* **BI Data Pipeline:** Scheduled data syncs to extract CRM snapshots into analytical data models for executive reporting.
