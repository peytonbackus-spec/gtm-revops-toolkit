# 02 — Siemens Ecosystem Positioning & Competitive Strategy

## Maya HTT Advantage: Partner Multiplier
Maya HTT is Siemens Digital Industries Software’s top global channel partner, providing a distinct co-sell advantage over standalone point solutions.

* **Software Attachment Rate:** Attach high-margin proprietary Maya HTT IP (EPIQ-M, M-Bot) to core Siemens licensing deals (Simcenter, NX, Teamcenter).
* **Co-Sell Synergy:** Position Maya HTT application engineers as an extension of the Siemens field sales team, unlocking joint account planning and co-marketing dollars.
* **Services Expansion:** Convert one-off software license renewals into high-value engineering services and AI custom development projects.
