# 01 — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Enterprise Industrial Targeting

## Target Segments
1. **Aerospace & Defense:** High-complexity simulation needs (thermal, CFD, structural).
2. **Automotive & EV Supply Chain:** Digital twin adoption, battery thermal management, battery testing.
3. **High-Tech & Electronics:** Electronics cooling (PCB Exchange, thermal model exchange).
4. **Industrial Equipment & Manufacturing:** Operational technology modernization, predictive maintenance, IIoT.

## Key Executive Personas
* **VP of Engineering / Chief Technology Officer:** Focused on reducing design cycle time, validation costs, and prototype physical tests.
* **VP of Manufacturing / Plant Operations:** Focused on OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness), reducing unplanned downtime, and AI vision quality control.
* **CIO / VP of Enterprise Applications:** Focused on PLM integration (Siemens Teamcenter), data silos, and cloud software governance.
