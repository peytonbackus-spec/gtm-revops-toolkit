# Content Engine: 1 Insight → 5 Touchpoints

Repurpose every client deliverable and technical setup into 5 collateral pieces without extra research:

1. **LinkedIn Post**: Tactical breakdown of the automation win (150–200 words).
2. **Loom Walkthrough**: 3-minute video showing the workflow in action.
3. **Blog Post**: Comprehensive 1,200+ word tactical guide (GEO optimized for LLM search engines).
4. **Newsletter Section**: Breakdown of ROI impact and code/prompt architecture.
5. **Community Answer**: Direct answer posted to RevOps/GTM developer communities.
