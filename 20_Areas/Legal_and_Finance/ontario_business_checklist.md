# Ontario Business & Tax Setup Checklist

- **Registration**: Ontario Business Registry ($60 sole proprietorship)[cite: 1]
- **Banking**: Dedicated business chequing account[cite: 1]
- **GST/HST**: Register with CRA once gross revenue exceeds $30K CAD / 4 consecutive quarters[cite: 1]
- **US Clients**: Zero-rated for GST/HST[cite: 1]
- **Tax Reserve**: Transfer 30% of every incoming client invoice immediately[cite: 1]
- **Legal Review**: Employment lawyer consult for contract/IP review[cite: 1]
