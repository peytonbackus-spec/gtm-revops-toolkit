# MEDDPICC Deal Inspection Framework

## Framework Components
- **Metrics**: Quantified ROI business case ($/time saved).
- **Economic Buyer**: Verified budget authority with line-item sign-off.
- **Decision Criteria**: Technical, commercial, and vendor evaluation rules.
- **Decision Process**: Evaluation, security, procurement, and legal steps.
- **Paper Process**: Contracting steps, MSA/SOW redlines, procurement timelines.
- **Identify Pain**: Explicit business impact caused by status quo.
- **Champion**: Internal advocate with power, influence, and personal stake.
- **Competitors**: Direct alternatives, internal build-vs-buy, or do-nothing risk.
