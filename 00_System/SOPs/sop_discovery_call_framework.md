# Discovery Call Framework (45 Minutes)

- **00–10m: Current State** — Tech stack, team layout, active GTM motions.
- **10–20m: Pain & Cost of Inaction** — Where are leads leaking? What is the cost of manual RevOps?
- **20–25m: What They've Tried** — Past agencies, internal hires, tools.
- **25–35m: Ideal Outcome** — Target revenue, pipeline targets, desired level of automation.
- **35–45m: Next Steps & Proposal** — Pitch $2,500 GTM Audit entry point. Commit to proposal within 24 hours.
