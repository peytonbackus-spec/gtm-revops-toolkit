# Sales Development Leadership

## Skill Tree
- Hiring & ramp plans for new BDR/SDR hires
- Activity & pipeline-generation metrics management (calls/emails/meetings-booked per rep)
- 1:1 coaching cadence and call-review frameworks
- Comp plan design for SDR/BDR teams
- Career ladder design (BDR -> Senior BDR -> AE track)

## Prompt Templates
**1:1 coaching agenda:**
```
Given this rep's activity metrics (calls, emails, meetings booked,
show rate) vs. team benchmarks, draft a 1:1 agenda that opens with
one specific strength, one specific gap tied to a metric, and one
concrete action for the coming week.
```
**30/60/90 ramp plan skeleton:**
```
Draft a 30/60/90 ramp plan for a new BDR: Days 1-30 (product/ICP/tools
certification), 31-60 (supervised outbound, first meetings booked),
61-90 (full quota ownership). Include specific activity and outcome
targets per phase.
```

## Execution Checklist — SDR/BDR Team Health
- [ ] Activity metrics tracked per rep against team benchmark (tie into [sales-development-strategy](sales-development-strategy.md) sequences)
- [ ] Ramp plan in place for every hire under 90 days tenure
- [ ] Weekly 1:1 coaching cadence maintained
- [ ] Comp plan reviewed against quota attainment distribution
- [ ] Career ladder criteria documented and communicated
