# Sales Leadership

## Skill Tree
- Territory & quota design
- Comp plan architecture (AE and BDR tiers)
- Forecasting methodology & commit process (tie into [account-executive](account-executive.md) deal health scoring)
- Board/exec revenue reporting
- Hiring & org design for AE and BDR functions

## Prompt Templates
**Forecast roll-up:**
```
Given per-rep deal health scores (see [meddpicc_health_engine](../../gtm-os/code/scoring/meddpicc_health_engine.md)) and
stage-weighted pipeline, draft a forecast commit/best-case/pipeline
breakdown with the top 3 risk factors called out by name.
```

## Execution Checklist — Quarterly Forecast & Org Review
- [ ] Territory/quota model checked against current headcount and pipeline capacity
- [ ] Comp plan attainment distribution reviewed for outliers
- [ ] Forecast commit built from deal-health scores, not gut-feel
- [ ] Board/exec summary drafted in plain-language, one page
