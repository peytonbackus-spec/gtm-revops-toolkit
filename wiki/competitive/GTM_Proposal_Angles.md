# 🎯 GTM Engineering Pitch Angles & Positioning

Targeted pitch frameworks for modernizing legacy stacks with agentic workflows and dynamic enrichment.

## 🛠️ 6sense

### ⚠️ Common Stack Vulnerabilities
- Contact tier pricing penalties and database bloat.
- Operational friction between marketing automation syncs and sales outreach.
- High manual intervention required for lead routing and scoring updates.

### 💡 Replacement & Modernization Angle
- Decouple static database storage into an agentic warehouse model.
- Use multi-provider waterfall routing (e.g., Clay + Frontier) to bypass seat-based data constraints.
- Trigger real-time, context-aware SDR engagement directly from signal spikes (e.g., RB2B/Koala).
