# Scope Management & Contract Requirements

## The rule on scope

Scope retainers as specific deliverables — never hours.
"3 automated sequences, 1 weekly report, 1 new workflow/month" prevents
scope creep. Hours invite abuse. Deliverables don't.

## Required contract clauses (every engagement)

1. **Scope of services** — explicit list of deliverables
2. **What's NOT included** — explicit exclusions list
3. **Payment terms** — 50% upfront for projects / monthly in advance for retainers
4. **Net-15 on remainder** — maximum. Never net-30 for a solo operator.
5. **30-day kill fee** — client pays 30 days notice to terminate
6. **IP ownership** — you own the methodology, client owns the outputs
7. **Refund policy** — no refunds after delivery begins (state explicitly)
8. **Change order process** — all out-of-scope work requires a signed change order
9. **Dispute resolution** — defined process before legal action
10. **Governing law** — Ontario, Canada

## Bad client protocols

### Scope creep
"Happy to look at that — it falls outside our current scope. Let me put
together a change order." Never do out-of-scope work without a signed
change order. One exception trains them to keep asking.

### Non-payment
- 7 days overdue: polite reminder
- 14 days: formal written notice referencing contract
- 21 days: pause all work
- 30 days: invoke dispute process

### Refund demand
"Per our agreement, refunds aren't available once work has commenced.
I'm happy to discuss what outcome you were expecting."

### Termination script
"I've reflected on our engagement and I don't think I'm the right fit
to deliver the results you're looking for. I'd like to invoke the
30-day termination clause. I'll complete [X] and provide a full handoff
document."
