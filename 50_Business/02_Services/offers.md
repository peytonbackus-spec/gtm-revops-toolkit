## Pricing rationale

A RevOps hire costs $150–200K/year fully loaded. The retainer at
$5,500–7,000/mo delivers equivalent or superior output through
automation leverage at roughly 40% of the cost. Frame every proposal
around this comparison, not around hourly rates.
