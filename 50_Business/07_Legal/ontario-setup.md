# Ontario Business Setup

## Sole proprietorship registration

- Register at: ontario.ca/businessregistry
- Cost: $60 CAD
- Time: ~20 minutes
- Valid: 5 years
- Required if operating under any name other than your legal name
- Run a name search first at the same site to avoid conflicts
- Also check: Canadian Trademarks Database (cipo.ic.gc.ca)

## Business bank account

Open same day as registration. Never mix personal and business finances.
Options: TD Business, RBC Business, Simplii Business (no-fee option).

## Professional email

Google Workspace: ~$8 USD/mo. Use yourname@yourdomain.com for all
client communications. Never use a personal Gmail for proposals.

## GST/HST

- Register with CRA once revenue hits $30,000 CAD over 4 consecutive quarters
- US clients: zero-rated (no GST/HST charged)
- Canadian clients: collect GST/HST and remit quarterly
- Register at: canada.ca/en/revenue-agency

## Tax set-aside

Set aside 30% of every payment in a dedicated account. CRA requires
quarterly installments once annual tax owed exceeds $3,000. Do not
use this money for operating expenses.

## Incorporation threshold

Consider incorporation above ~$80K/yr net income:
- Personal asset protection
- Tax advantages (corporate rate vs personal rate)
- SR&ED credits may apply to AI development work
- Talk to a CA when approaching this threshold

## Insurance

Professional liability (E&O) insurance: ~$600–900 CAD/year.
Essential once building automations that touch client revenue systems.
Providers: Hiscox Canada, Aviva, BFL Canada.

## Private health insurance (if no employer plan)

Ontario Blue Cross individual plans: $80–250 CAD/mo depending on
coverage. Purchase immediately — do not go uninsured.
