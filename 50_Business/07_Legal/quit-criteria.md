# When to Stop — Explicit Quit Criteria

Evaluate these on the first of each month. Use only data, not feelings.

## Stop if ALL THREE are true simultaneously

1. **Personal runway under 6 weeks** with no revenue imminent
2. **90+ days of genuine effort** (20+ contacts/day) with zero paid revenue
3. **The work creates sustained dread** — not normal difficulty, but genuine
   dread of outreach, calls, and delivery for 2+ consecutive weeks

All three together = market, model, or fit is not working.
Stop. Take a good job. This is not failure — it is data.

## Adjust (don't stop) if only one or two are true

One or two conditions being true signals a change of approach, not exit:
- Change the offer
- Change the ICP / vertical
- Change the pricing
- Change the outreach channel

## The honest timeline

- Most solo consultants reach $10K/mo between month 3 and month 6
- Almost none achieve it in 30 days regardless of any plan
- Plan for month 4. Hope for month 2. Evaluate honestly at month 6.
- $5K+/mo at month 6 with upward trajectory = stay in
- Zero at month 6 with genuine effort = exit cleanly

## What "stopping" actually means

Stopping this iteration does not mean giving up on the skills or direction.
Many people who pause consulting take a role doing the same work inside a
company, build proof there, and relaunch 18 months later — with case
studies, a clearer offer, and a network of potential clients.
The skills compound regardless of the outcome.
