## Unit economics per retainer client

- Revenue: $5,500–7,000 CAD/mo
- Delivery time (with automations): 4–6 hrs/week oversight
- Effective implied rate: $210–400+ CAD/hr
- Contribution margin (after infrastructure): ~$5,000–6,500/mo

## Break-even

One $5,500 retainer covers operating costs + survival number for most
people. Two clients = fully funded. Three clients = at $20K target.

## Cash flow rule

**Always invoice 50% upfront.** Invoice-to-cash gap is real:
net-15 means cash arrives 2–4 weeks after the contract is signed.
Without 50% upfront, month 1 cash received could be $0 even if
$10K was invoiced.

## Tax set-aside

Set aside 30% of every payment immediately into a separate account.
CRA requires quarterly installments once annual tax owed exceeds $3,000.
This account is untouchable.
