cover all essential expenses. This is the floor, not the target.

## Runway calculation

```
Runway (months) = Savings ÷ Monthly expenses
```

| Runway | Mode |
|--------|------|
| Under 8 weeks | Emergency — job search starts NOW in parallel |
| 8–16 weeks | Urgent — every day counts, no slack in execution |
| 4–6 months | Normal build mode |
| 6+ months | Comfortable build with room to be deliberate |

## The 8-week rule

Never let runway drop below 8 weeks without an active job search
running in parallel. Job searches take 4–8 weeks. You cannot job
search and build a business simultaneously with full attention.
