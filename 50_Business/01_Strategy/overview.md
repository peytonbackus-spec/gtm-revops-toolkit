# Business Overview & Niche

## The concept

An AI-native GTM and revenue operations consulting firm that builds and
runs autonomous pipeline systems for B2B SaaS companies with 10–150
employees. These companies are post-early-stage, have real budget, but
cannot yet justify a $150–200K/year RevOps hire.

## Positioning

> "I build autonomous revenue systems for B2B SaaS companies that need
> a full GTM engine — without the $200K/year RevOps hire. Powered by
> AI. Run by me."

## Why this works

- Most consultants are either technical (can build, bad at sales) or
  strategic (good at decks, can't execute)
- Strong combination of sales development + RevOps + GTM engineering
- Claude Code enables solo delivery at multi-person output

## Key metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Monthly net revenue | $20,000 CAD |
| Clients required | 3–4 retainers |
| Average retainer | $5,500–7,000 CAD/mo |
| Timeline (worst case) | Month 6 |

## Target market

**Primary:** Series A–B SaaS companies, 10–80 employees, recently funded,
no dedicated RevOps hire yet. CEO or CRO still running GTM.

**Secondary:** Professional services firms (law, accounting, consulting)
modernising their sales motion. Budget available, less competitive.
