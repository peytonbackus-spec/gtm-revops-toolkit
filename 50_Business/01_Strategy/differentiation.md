# Differentiation & Wedge Strategy

## The problem

"I build AI-powered revenue systems using Clay and HubSpot" is saturated
positioning. Thousands of consultants use a variation of this pitch.
Volume of outreach will not fix a positioning problem.

## Three wedge options — pick ONE for days 1–90

### Wedge A: Vertical specialisation
Pick one industry within B2B SaaS. Become "the GTM consultant for [vertical]."
- Options: HR tech, fintech, dev tools, legal tech, climate tech
- Advantage: premium pricing, faster close, buyers feel understood
- How to choose: which vertical did you work in? Which is actively funded?

### Wedge B: Outcome guarantee
Sell "30 qualified meetings in 60 days or we keep going until you get them."
- Advantage: eliminates price shopping, attracts decisive buyers
- Risk: requires confidence in delivery before case studies exist
- Best after: first case study validates the outcome is achievable

### Wedge C: ICP focus (recommended for day 1)
Only serve founder-led companies where the CEO is still doing sales.
- Pitch: "I get B2B SaaS founders off the sales treadmill in 30 days"
- Advantage: identifiable on LinkedIn, universal pain, fast discovery calls
- Signal: CEO bio mentions "growing" or "selling" the business
