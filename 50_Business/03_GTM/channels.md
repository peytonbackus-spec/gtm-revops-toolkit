# Go-to-Market Channels

## Channel priority (in order)

| Channel | Time to first close | Required volume | Priority |
|---------|--------------------|--------------------|----------|
| Warm network (DMs) | 1–2 weeks | 30+ direct messages | 🔴 First |
| LinkedIn outreach (signal-based) | 4–6 weeks | 20 messages/day | 🔴 Week 2+ |
| LinkedIn content (3x/week) | 90 days to compound | 3 posts/week | 🟡 Start now |
| Cold email via Apollo | 5–8 weeks | 50+/week | 🟡 Week 3+ |
| Referral programme (10% fee) | Ongoing | Activate after client 1 | 🟢 Month 2 |
| HubSpot Solutions Partner | 60–90 days after joining | Inbound | 🟢 Month 3+ |
| Case study + inbound | Month 3+ | 2 published studies | 🟢 Month 3+ |

## The real conversion math

- Cold email to close: ~0.2% end-to-end (1 deal per 464 emails)
- LinkedIn reply rate: 10–25% (reply ≠ booked call ≠ close)
- Touchpoints to close: 5–12 (48% of people never send follow-up #2)
- Consulting cold conversion: ~2% of qualified conversations

**Implication:** To close 1 client per month via cold outreach alone,
you need ~50 conversations. To book 50, you need ~500 contacts reached.
Warm network is the only channel that closes fast enough in month 1.

## 3x pipeline coverage rule

Always maintain 3x your target in active proposals.
- Want $20K/mo? Need $60K in proposed work simultaneously.
- Want 3 clients? Need 9 active proposal conversations.
