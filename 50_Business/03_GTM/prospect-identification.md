# Prospect Identification System

## Lead sources (ranked by quality)

### 1. LinkedIn Sales Navigator ($99 USD/mo)
**Filters:**
- Company headcount: 11–150
- Industry: Software / SaaS
- Geography: Canada, United States
- Title: CEO, CRO, Co-founder, VP Sales, Head of Revenue, VP Growth
- Funding: Series A, Series B

### 2. Apollo.io (free tier → $49/mo)
**Key filter:** Technologies used = HubSpot
Companies using HubSpot with no dedicated RevOps admin = warmest cold target.

### 3. Crunchbase / BetaKit
- Crunchbase: funding alerts for Series A/B, B2B SaaS, 10–100 employees
- BetaKit (betakit.com): Canadian tech funding, check weekly
- LinkedIn: search "excited to announce Series A" — warm for 2 weeks post-announcement

### 4. Clay (automates all of the above)
Build a table that pulls from:
- Crunchbase new funding webhook
- Apollo enrichment (email + title)
- LinkedIn (check for RevOps/SDR titles on team)
- Built With (confirm HubSpot in tech stack)
- Claude API (write personalised first line)
- Output: push to HubSpot with task to send outreach

### 5. Free signal sources
- TechCrunch Funding Roundup (weekly)
- Y Combinator company list (filter 2022–2024 B2B, active)
- Product Hunt (launches in last 6 months)
- Pavilion, RevGenius, Slack communities

## Buying signals (prioritise prospects with 3+)

| Signal | Why it matters |
|--------|----------------|
| 🔥 Raised Series A/B in last 90 days | Fresh capital + board pressure = urgency |
| 🔥 Hiring VP Sales or Head of Revenue | No sales leader yet = clear opening |
| 🔥 Founder still doing sales | The exact pain you solve |
| 🔥 HubSpot in stack, no HubSpot admin | Tool exists, nobody running it |
| ⚡ Hired first 1–3 AEs | Sales reps with no system = chaos |
| ⚡ Posting about pipeline/outbound pain | Self-identified buyer |
| ⚡ New CRO/VP Sales hired in last 90 days | New leader rebuilding the stack |
| 🌡 15–60 employees | Sweet spot: enough revenue, not enough ops |

## Prospect scoring

| Score | Profile | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| HOT | Series A <90 days + founder doing sales + HubSpot + 15–60 employees | Same-day outreach |
| WARM | Series A/B 6–18 months + hiring sales/SDRs + <80 employees | 48-hour outreach |
| COLD | Series A/B, no obvious timing signal, >80 employees | Add to sequence, lower priority |

## Who to contact (title priority)

1. **Founder / CEO (10–50 employees)** — fastest close, budget authority
2. **CRO / VP Sales hired <90 days** — highest ACV, most urgent
3. **VP Growth / Head of Growth** — good retainer clients, understand automation
4. **Head of Marketing / CMO** — good for referrals to #1 and #2

**Avoid for first outreach:** VP Engineering, CTO, individual SDRs/AEs (not decision-makers)
