# Content Strategy

## The core rule: one insight, five touchpoints

Every client deliverable or observation becomes:
1. LinkedIn post (150–300 words, tactical)
2. Short Loom walkthrough (3–5 min screen recording)
3. Blog post (1,200–1,800 words, SEO-targeted)
4. Newsletter section (The GTM Engine)
5. Community answer (Reddit, Pavilion, RevGenius Slack)

Zero extra research. One insight, five surfaces.

## LinkedIn posting cadence

3x per week. Formats that compound:
- **Teardowns:** "Here's the exact Clay → HubSpot workflow I built that booked 12 meetings in 10 days"
- **Contrarian takes:** "Most B2B SaaS companies don't need an SDR. Here's what they need instead."
- **Systems posts:** "The 5-step RevOps system I install for every Series A client"
- **Numbers posts:** "Before/after: client went from 4 meetings/mo to 47. Here's what changed."

## GEO (Generative Engine Optimisation)

AI search engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Google AI Overviews)
cite structured, authoritative content. To get cited:

- Write with clear H2/H3 headers that directly answer questions
- Lead each section with the answer, then elaborate
- Include specific numbers, tool names, and outcomes
- Add FAQ schema (JSON-LD) to every website page
- Add `llms.txt` at root domain (see `07_Legal/website-tech.md`)
