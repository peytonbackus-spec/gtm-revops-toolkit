# Outreach Templates

## The formula
1 line personalisation → their specific pain → your relevant proof → soft CTA

Never lead with what you do. Lead with what you noticed about them.

---

## Template 1 — Just raised funding (hottest signal)

**LinkedIn connection request note:**
```
Congrats on the [Series A/B] — saw the announcement on [BetaKit/TechCrunch].
Most teams at your stage are now under pressure to build a repeatable pipeline
motion fast. I build autonomous GTM systems for B2B SaaS founders — curious
if that's on your radar right now?
```

---

## Template 2 — Founder doing sales

**LinkedIn DM (after connecting):**
```
Hey [Name] — noticed you're running both the product and sales side at
[Company]. I work with B2B SaaS founders at exactly your stage to build
the GTM system that gets you off the direct-selling treadmill — using
Clay, HubSpot, and AI automation. Would a 20-min call be worth it to
see if there's a fit?
```

---

## Template 3 — Hiring VP Sales or SDRs

**Email or LinkedIn DM:**
```
Hey [Name] — saw [Company] is hiring a [VP Sales / SDR]. Quick question
while that search is in progress: do you have the RevOps infrastructure
for them to land in? Most companies at your stage don't — and a new hire
without a system underperforms in the first 90 days.

I build the automated pipeline and CRM systems that make new sales hires
2x as productive from day one. Happy to share what that looks like in a
20-min call if the timing is right.
```

## Template 4 — New CRO/VP Sales (target them directly)

**LinkedIn DM:**
```
Hey [Name] — congrats on the new role at [Company]. Most revenue leaders
in the first 60 days are rebuilding the stack and trying to show early
pipeline wins. I specialise in building autonomous GTM systems for
exactly this moment — Clay, HubSpot, AI outbound. Have a few ideas
specific to [Company]'s motion. Worth a quick call?
```

## Template 5 — LinkedIn post → DM

**Comment on their post first:**
```
This is the exact challenge I see at every Series A/B company right now —
the manual work compounds as the team grows. [Add specific tactical insight]
```

**DM 24 hours later:**
```
Hey [Name] — commented on your post about [topic]. We work with B2B SaaS
leaders on exactly this — building automated pipeline infrastructure so
the team isn't doing everything manually. If you want to compare notes,
happy to do a 20-min call.
```

---

## Template 6 — Cold email

**Subject: GTM system for [Company]**
```
Hey [Name],

Noticed [Company] raised [round] in [month] — congrats. At your stage,
the biggest growth bottleneck is usually pipeline infrastructure: no
automated outbound, manual CRM, and a founder or small team doing the
heavy lifting.

I build autonomous GTM systems for B2B SaaS companies — Clay + HubSpot
+ AI — that generate qualified pipeline without adding headcount. My last
client went from 0 to 40+ meetings/month in 8 weeks.

Worth a 20-min call to see if there's a fit? I have time [Tuesday or
Thursday].

[Your name]
```

## Follow-up cadence

| Touch | Timing | Channel | Message |
|-------|--------|---------|---------|
| 1 | Day 0 | LinkedIn DM or email | Personalised opening (above) |
| 2 | Day 3 | Same channel | "Just bumping this up — did you get a chance to see my note?" |
| 3 | Day 7 | Switch channel | If DM → try email. Share relevant content as hook. |
| 4 | Day 14 | LinkedIn | "Last note — if timing changes, grab time here: [Calendly]" |
