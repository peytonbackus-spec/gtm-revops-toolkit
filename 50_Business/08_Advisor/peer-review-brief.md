# Peer Review Brief — Open Questions

This document summarises open questions for founder peer review sessions.
Not looking for validation — looking for the specific things that are
wrong, missing, or that someone outside the plan would spot immediately.

## Offer and pricing

**Q: Is $2,500 the right audit price for a Series A founder?**
Context: Concern that too low signals low value. Counter-concern that
too high without proof creates friction. The audit is designed as a
loss-leader to the retainer.

**Q: Does the $5,500–7,000 retainer hold against the current market?**
Context: Comparable fractional RevOps services range from $3,000–10,000/mo.
Looking for where the market actually transacts vs. what people claim to charge.

**Q: Should there be a performance-based component?**
Context: An outcome guarantee ("30 qualified meetings in 60 days or we
keep going") would sharpen positioning but requires confidence in delivery
before case studies exist. Timing question more than philosophy question.

## Positioning and wedge

**Q: Which of the three wedge strategies would you bet on?**
- Wedge A: Vertical specialisation (one industry, e.g. HR tech or fintech)
- Wedge B: Outcome guarantee ("30 meetings in 60 days")
- Wedge C: ICP focus ("I get B2B SaaS founders off the sales treadmill")
- Or a fourth option?

**Q: What does the content engine need to look like to actually drive inbound?**
Context: 3 LinkedIn posts/week is the plan. What format and angle compounds
fastest for a B2B services firm — teardowns, case study threads, opinion pieces?

## Go-to-market

**Q: What's the fastest path to a warm referral for someone with an existing
professional network launching a consulting firm?**
Context: Plan uses direct DMs to former colleagues. Is there a higher-leverage
ask structure, event, or approach?

**Q: Where would you look for the first client if you were doing this?**
Not the playbook answer — what would you actually do in the first 2 weeks
based on how you built your first client base?

## Reality check

**Q: What's the thing this plan is most wrong about?**
No defensive framing. What assumption will prove false? What market dynamic
is being missed?

**Q: At what point would you tell a friend to stop and go back to employment?**
Current quit criteria: zero revenue at month 3 with genuine effort + runway
under 6 weeks + sustained dread — all three simultaneously. Does this feel
right from your experience building a firm?

## SEO-specific questions (for SEO founder peer)

**Q: Is there a faster path to SEO credibility in a saturated niche?**
Thinking: guest posts on HubSpot Blog, Clay community, Apollo blog, Pavilion.
Strategic backlinks vs. content volume. What's the actual leverage point?

**Q: Is GEO (AI search optimisation) a real acquisition channel for B2B
services yet, or still too early?**
Context: Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, and Google AI Overviews are all pulling
from structured web content. Is it worth investing in this specifically, or
is it noise for the next 12 months?

**Q: What content angle have you seen compound fastest for a solo B2B
services consultant?**
Tactical system teardowns, case study threads, contrarian opinion pieces,
or something else entirely?
